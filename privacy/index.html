Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Twolight, an app by Christian Almanza ("we", "us"), handles your information. Questions: support@twolightapp.com.

The short version

  • We don't collect what you put in Twolight. We have no servers that store it, and we can't see it. The one thing we keep is a random security key ID for each install, to block forged notifications (see Notifications between partners). It isn't linked to you.
  • Your information stays on your iPhone and in iCloud, through Apple, encrypted on your iPhone before it's stored. Your private things go to your own iCloud; what you share with your partner goes to a space only the two of you can open.
  • Your partner sees only what Twolight shares between partners, and only after you approve them by name.
  • Your location is never sent to your partner, and never to us.
  • Notifications to your partner pass through a small service we run that hands them to Apple and keeps no record of them. They say who sent what kind of thing, never the words you wrote.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your information.
  • Apple Intelligence features run on your iPhone. What you write isn't sent anywhere to use them.

What goes to Apple

Twolight uses Apple's own services, which Apple handles under its privacy policy:

  • iCloud (CloudKit) keeps a copy of your information (never your home, work or any location) in your own iCloud account and, once you pair, syncs what you share between your two phones. The shared space lives in the iCloud of whoever sent the invite, and only the two of you can open it. What you write is encrypted on your iPhone before it's stored, with keys in your iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encryption), so neither we nor Apple can read it; Apple can see basic details, like how much is stored and when it changed. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Apple Maps receives the search (for example "pizza") and an approximate area when you use Surprise us, to find places nearby. When you set home or work, it receives the address you search for, or the point you pick on the map, to show you the street address. When you tap Heading home, Heading to work or head somewhere else, it also receives where you are at that moment and where you're going (your home or work point, or the place you picked), once, to estimate the drive with current traffic. When you head somewhere else, it also receives what you search for. If a favorite is linked to a place (like Starbucks), then when you open Twolight it receives where you are, once, to see if you're at one; nothing runs while the app is closed. If you choose to open directions (the Apple Maps button after you head out, Settings → Open Apple Maps, or saying "Drive home with Twolight" to Siri), Twolight opens the Apple Maps app with your destination: your home or work point, or the place you picked. If you turn on Live updates on the way, it receives where you are every few minutes during that trip only, to keep the estimate current. Your partner sees only the estimated arrival time, never where you are.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers the notifications your partner's actions send you (see Notifications between partners, below).
  • App Attest lets your iPhone prove to our notification service that requests come from the real Twolight app.
  • Siri handles your voice when you use a Twolight shortcut.
  • The App Store handles subscriptions and payments. We never see your payment details.

What your partner sees

Once you pair and approve them, your partner sees what Twolight shares between partners: your name, profile photo and love language (if you set them), your work hours and day changes, chores between you, hugs, kisses, praise and wake notes, your talk light, running late, heading home or heading to work (with when you'll arrive, and when you get there) or heading somewhere else (with the place you share, if any: its name and street, which you can change or clear before sending), your energy status if you set one, the grocery list, date plans, the shared plans you add (family events, outings, appointments) and whether they've seen them, whether you've opened the hugs, kisses, praise and notes they sent you, favorites about yourself that you choose to share (like your coffee order), any reflection you choose to share (with its photos, videos and voice memos), and, if you subscribe to Plus, when your subscription ends (so it covers them too). They never see your location, the favorites and gift ideas you keep about them, your private reflections, your journal, or your check-in notes. Either of you can unpair at any time, which stops future sharing; it can't take back what was already seen. When either of you unpairs, what the other person wrote is removed from the shared space and from your iPhone, except the hugs, kisses, praise and notes they sent you, which stay with you. Your own things stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

Notifications between partners

When you send your partner something, like a hug, a chore or a change to your talk light, Twolight asks a small service we run on Cloudflare to hand a notification to Apple, which delivers it to their iPhone. The notification says only who it's from and what kind of thing it is (for example "Sam sent you a hug"), never the words you wrote. If you share where you're headed, the place is sealed on your iPhone so that only your partner's iPhone can open it; our service, Cloudflare and Apple can't read it. Your partner can also choose to hide the place on their Lock Screen until their iPhone is unlocked.

Our service keeps no record of notifications: no log, no copy, and nothing about who sent what to whom. Your IP address is used for a moment to limit abuse, held in a counter that clears within a minute, and isn't stored. The address Apple uses to reach your partner's iPhone is kept in the space you share, not by us. To make sure requests come from the real Twolight app, each install registers a key through Apple's App Attest. We keep that key's public half and its random ID, with a counter and the date it was last used, so each request can be checked. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple Account or anything you write, and it's deleted after 180 days without use. In the App Store's privacy terms this is a device identifier, used only for app functionality (security), not linked to you and never used for tracking.

Your journal

The journal is just for you. Your pages, the mood you pick, and their photos and videos are never shared with your partner. The words, mood, photos and videos are also kept in your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted, never in the space you share. The mood is only a record: Twolight doesn't analyze it, react to it or use it for anything. You can back up everything at any time (Settings → Back up everything) as a file that opens in any web browser and can be restored into Twolight.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, Twolight can suggest other ways to word a note, sort a chore from a sentence, make a grocery list, and write a few lines looking back on your year. These run on your iPhone's built-in model; what you write isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Suggestions only fill in a box for you to edit; nothing is sent to your partner unless you send it.

Subscriptions

Twolight Plus is sold by Apple through your Apple ID. Apple tells us a subscription is active; we never receive your name, payment details or purchase history.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • Show ads, or use advertising or analytics tools.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.
  • Share your location with your partner, or keep it ourselves.
  • Read, monitor or review anything you write, including your messages, reflections and journal. We have no way to.

Your choices and deleting your information

  • Delete one thing: chores, favorites, reflections, notes and check-ins can each be deleted in the app. A note for later can be taken back until it arrives; after that, deleting it removes it from your Notes, and your partner keeps their copy.
  • Start over: Settings → Start over erases everything on this phone and your private copy in iCloud.
  • Delete the app: erases what's on this phone only. Your copy in iCloud stays until you remove it (below), so it's there if you come back: when you install Twolight again, or on a new iPhone, it asks before bringing it back. Home and work aren't in iCloud and don't come back.
  • Keep a copy: Settings → Back up everything saves all your Twolight information, including your journal, gift ideas, photos and videos, as a file you choose where to keep, and Restore from a copy puts it back. Backups aren't encrypted by Twolight, and once saved they're under your control: keep them somewhere private, like iCloud Drive.
  • Shared information: unpair in Settings to stop sharing. Your partner's things are removed as described in What your partner sees.
  • Remove what's in iCloud: in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud, manage Twolight's storage and delete its data.
  • Invites: cancel one any time on the invite screen; it's deleted from iCloud. Unanswered invites are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Check-in notes are kept only as long as you choose (Past check-ins → Keep notes for).

Because we don't hold your information, there's nothing for us to send you or delete on our side. If you have a question, write to support@twolightapp.com.

Children

Twolight is for adults 18 and older. It isn't meant for children, and we don't knowingly let anyone under 18 use it.

Security

Your information on the phone is protected by iOS's built-in encryption whenever your iPhone has a passcode, which iOS needs for Face ID and Touch ID. If you don't use a passcode, we recommend setting one. Most Siri shortcuts need an unlocked phone. The ones for the road (heading home or to work, a quick stop) also work while it's locked, so they work from CarPlay; they only tell your partner you're on your way. Opening directions in Apple Maps still needs your iPhone unlocked. Copied favorites stay on that phone and clear after two minutes. What you write, and your photos, videos and voice memos, are encrypted on your iPhone before they go to iCloud, so only your devices (and, for what you share, your partner's) can open them. If your iCloud is full, nothing is lost: it waits on your iPhone until there's room.

Changes to this policy

If we change what Twolight does with your information, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
