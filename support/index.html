Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email support@twolightapp.com. A real person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

Twolight isn't monitored and isn't an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. In the US you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Common questions

A little help with pairing, your memories and everything in between.

* Coming soon in an update to Twolight. Questions marked with an asterisk are about the next version.

How do I pair with my partner?

Both of you need Twolight, an internet connection and your own iCloud accounts. Open Invite your partner, send the invitation, and have your partner open it and request to join. The person who sent the invitation then approves the request by name. Check that it is your partner before approving. “Just looking around?” loads a sample partner for exploring; it does not connect to another person.

What happens when I unpair?

Unpairing ends your shared connection and removes partner content according to what belongs to each of you. Your own content stays. Received hugs and letters, and a past date your partner proposed that you wrote a reflection on, are kept. It does not erase copies your partner has already kept. Before unpairing, both people should save a backup if they want to keep their current view. You can invite each other again, but re-pairing is not an undo button for deleted content.

What should I choose after reinstalling?

Before deleting the app, save a backup outside it. After reinstalling, sign into the same iCloud account. If Twolight offers your earlier iCloud copy, choose Bring it back to restore it and keep the pairing it can recover. Only content that reached iCloud can come back this way.

Start fresh is different: it ends the old pairing when Twolight can verify it belongs to that copy. It is not the choice for staying paired. If the copy is missing or there is an error, pause and contact support rather than repeatedly reinstalling or choosing Start fresh.

Can I switch Apple IDs?

Make a backup first: in Settings > Your data, choose Back up. Save it somewhere you can still access after changing accounts, such as a Mac. If possible, unpair while still signed into the old account so its shared connection can be ended there.

After switching, Twolight pauses sync rather than silently uploading to a different account. To make the change permanent, open Twolight Settings and choose Use this account under “A different iCloud account”. Your content stays on the phone, but the old pairing ends locally; you will need to pair again. This does not clean up the old account’s iCloud share. Use Restore in Settings > Your data if you need to recover content from your saved backup; it replaces the current content and does not restore the old pairing or transfer an Apple subscription. Switching accounts is not a seamless transfer of a couple’s shared space.

Which dates and memories can my partner see?

Dates you log yourself start private. Share one from its memory screen when you want your partner to see the date. Sharing a date and sharing what you wrote about it are separate choices: your reflection stays private unless you share it too. Dates planned together are shared.

Stop sharing makes your own logged date private again. If your partner has written a reflection on that date, their copy can stay with their reflection. Stopping sharing cannot take back something they already saved. Deleting a private logged date removes it; removing a shared past date can hide it only on your phone.

What does “Cloud deletions paused” mean?

Twolight could not read some saved content, or a restored copy was missing media your items refer to. It pauses cloud deletions so missing local content is not mistaken for something you intentionally removed. Uploads can continue, but removing something locally will not remove its iCloud or partner copy while the hold is active. Stop sharing is also blocked.

Use Restore with a complete, readable backup that includes the needed media. A successful complete restore clears the hold. A partial backup, or a new backup made after content went missing, may not clear it. If you do not have a complete copy, keep what you have and contact support; do not delete the app just to remove the warning.

How do I make and restore a backup?

Open Settings > Your data > Back up and save the ZIP outside Twolight. Keep the original ZIP; the copy is not password-protected and contains personal content, so store it privately.

To restore, unzip it in Files, then choose Settings > Your data > Restore. Select the extracted backup folder containing twolight-data.json and its media folder, not the JSON file alone. Read the replacement confirmation before continuing. Save a fresh copy of your current data first.

Restoring replaces the content currently on this phone; changes made after that backup may be lost. Pairing and the current iCloud account stay as they are now. If paired, Twolight fetches your partner’s current shared content again. A backup does not reverse cloud deletions or unpairing.

Why aren’t notifications arriving?

Check that both phones have an internet connection, are still paired and have the current Twolight version. On the receiving iPhone, check Settings > Notifications > Twolight and any Focus settings that might silence it. In Twolight, check Tell me when and the kinds of alerts you want enabled. Live Activities also need to be allowed.

Is iCloud having a problem? Twolight syncs through Apple's iCloud. If Apple is having an outage, or your iPhone keeps asking you to sign in to your Apple Account, sync and partner alerts may be delayed. Check Apple System Status for iCloud. Keep the app and its local data in place, and try again after Apple reports that service is restored. Don't unpair or restore to "fix" it.

Some messages wait while your partner is expected to be asleep, based on their schedule. Red talk requests bypass Twolight’s sleep hold, but cannot override disabled notifications or a network problem. The sample partner does not send real push notifications. If alerts still fail, send support the time, both app versions and what you tried; leave private messages out of screenshots.

Does Plus cover both of us? How do I restore it?

Yes. One active Twolight Plus subscription covers both paired partners. You do not both need to buy it. On the purchaser’s phone, open Settings > Twolight Plus and use Restore Purchases, with the App Store account that bought it. Make sure both phones are online and paired so the partner’s access can update. Restoring purchases does not restore journal entries, memories or a backup.

How do I cancel Plus?

On the purchasing iPhone, open Apple’s Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Twolight, then choose Cancel Subscription if shown. Apple shows the subscription’s end date. Deleting Twolight or unpairing does not cancel it. See Apple’s cancellation instructions.

What does the hidden talk light hide?

It hides the urgency on the Lock Screen: the light is gray instead of showing green, yellow or red. Your partner can still see that you want to talk and open Twolight to see the color. It does not hide the request itself.

How does the wish list work? Will my partner see what I bought?* (coming soon in an update)

Add things you'd love in Favorites > Me > Wish list. Shared items show up in your partner's gift ideas. When they mark one bought or given, that stays on their phone only: you never see it, so the surprise stays a surprise. If you delete an item they already bought, they keep it on their side. Both of you need Twolight 1.1.

Where do bought groceries go?* (coming soon in an update)

Ticked items stay on the list for the rest of the day, then move to Bought below, grouped by month and kept for a year. Search finds things on the list and things you bought before; tap a bought item to add it again. For the full history, both of you need Twolight 1.1.

Can I save places for trips?* (coming soon in an update)

With Plus, yes: up to 5 (the gym, a parent's house) in Heading somewhere. They stay on your phone, like Home and Work, so they don't come back after reinstalling the app. Your partner only sees the name you chose, during a trip.

How do I get back a date I removed?* (coming soon in an update)

Removed past dates go to Dates > Recently removed, with Put back. Choose how long they stay listed with Show removed dates for, in Recently removed. Removing a date only hides it on your phone; it's never deleted for your partner.

How do I send flowers?* (coming soon in an update)

On Today, tap Flowers, choose your stems and wrapping, and add a note if you like. Tap Wrap it up, then Send. Your partner opens the bouquet in their Mailbox and can reread it any time. Free includes one bouquet every seven days; Plus lets you send flowers as often as you like. Both of you need Twolight 1.1.

How do love letters work? Can my partner read one early?* (coming soon in an update)

On Today, tap Write a love letter, pick the stationery and wax seal, and choose how it travels. Standard arrives in 2–3 days and is free; Express (tonight) and the scenic route (about a week), plus more stationery and seals, come with Plus. Your partner sees it’s on its way and when it will arrive, but can’t open it until then. Once it’s sent, it can’t be unsent. Letters stay in the Mailbox to reread. Both of you need Twolight 1.1.

How do I turn on a holiday border?* (coming soon in an update)

With Plus, open Settings > Look & sound > Today border and pick one: Thanksgiving, Christmas, Valentine’s, Halloween, New Year or Spring, or Match the season to change with the calendar. It decorates the edges of Today only and never covers your text. With Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode on, it stays still.

Can Twolight fill in a gift’s title and price from a link?* (coming soon in an update)

Yes, on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. At the top of a gift idea or wish list item, tap Paste (or type the link and tap Load link). Twolight fills in a short title, the price and the product picture, only where you haven’t already added something, and removes long tracking codes from shop links. If the picture isn’t right, remove it or pick your own. Check everything before saving, or tap Undo fill. The price is saved as the price when you added it. Some shops block apps from reading their pages; then you’ll see “Couldn’t read that page”: fix the link and tap Try again, or type them in. Your iPhone opens only the page you entered and reads it on the phone. Links shared from shopping apps (for example Abercrombie's) work too: Twolight uses the product name and picture carried in the link. Without Apple Intelligence, type the price yourself, and the title too if the link doesn't carry one.

How do countdowns on Today work?* (coming soon in an update)

The top of Today shows your countdowns, soonest first: your anniversary, your partner’s birthday, your own dates (up to 8, every year or one time, like a trip or a child’s birthday) and any plan you pin with Count down on Today. Holidays are optional. Up to 4 show at once; with more, swipe sideways through up to 3 pages. To keep one in front no matter how far away it is, long-press it and choose Keep on the first page, or use Settings → Countdowns. Your own countdowns and pins stay private to you.

Can my partner see my countdowns?* (coming soon in an update)

No, unless you ask. On a plan, turn on Ask your partner to count down too. They choose Add to my Today or Not now; if they say not now, it waits under Shared by on their Today until the plan’s day, in case they change their mind. You see a note when they add it, never when they don’t.

Can I rearrange Today?* (coming soon in an update)

Yes. In Settings → Today layout, drag sections into the order you like or switch off the ones you don’t use. It’s just for your iPhone; your partner keeps their own. Update notices, your partner’s live trip and favorites nearby always stay at the top. Reset to default puts everything back.

My partner has Plus. Do I need it too?* (coming soon in an update)

No. One subscription covers you both, so the Plus screen shows that you’re covered and has nothing to buy. If both of you already pay, it tells you so and lets you manage the subscription so one of you can cancel.

Can I add a gift straight from Safari or a shopping app?* (coming soon in an update)

Yes. Tap the app's Share button and choose Twolight (if you don't see it, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More and turn it on). Pick My wish list or Gift idea for your partner, and tap Add. Next time you open Twolight, it's added and the details fill in; you'll see a note with Undo if you change your mind. Gift ideas stay private to you, as always.

Is there a tour of the app?* (coming soon in an update)

Yes. A short tour shows you around the first time you open Today: each tab, the Mailbox, Favorites and the wish list, and what swiping left does. To see it again, open Settings > Help & about > Take the tour.

How does Twolight protect our privacy?

Your journal is private, and you choose which logged dates and reflections to share. Content syncs through Apple’s iCloud using encrypted fields, and media is encrypted before upload. Notifications pass through a relay that retains a per-install security identifier, not a stored history of your messages; some notification names and kinds are visible in transit to the services delivering them. Background location is used only during a trip you start with live updates enabled, with the blue iOS indicator; foreground location also supports place setup and nearby suggestions.
