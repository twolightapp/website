Private by design

No ads, no tracking, and no account with us. Your information stays on your iPhone, and only what you share goes to your partner, through your own iCloud. Your partner never sees where you are. Only Apple Maps does, to find places and drive times. Apple Intelligence features run on the device and only ever suggest; you're always the one who sends. Read the privacy policy.
