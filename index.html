BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few good questions.

All questions & support ↗
Do we both need Plus?

No. One active subscription covers both paired partners. Schedules, chores, the talk light and the everyday essentials stay free.

Can I look around on my own?

Yes. Choose “Just looking around?” on the invite screen to explore with a sample partner. Real sharing and partner notifications need a second person and their iPhone.

Will my partner see my location?

They see your arrival estimate, not a live location map. When heading somewhere else, you choose the destination details you share. Live background updates only run during a trip you start with the feature enabled.

How do I keep a copy?

In Settings, choose Back up everything and save the ZIP somewhere private outside the app. See our backup and restore guide before reinstalling or changing accounts.
